## The Kitchen
Parlay Lab is now The Kitchen.

Two stations are available:
- Home Run Parlays: the existing Dinger Score builder, locks, and rerolls.
- Mixed Prop Parlays: combines Total Bases, Hits, Pitcher Strikeouts, and Stolen Bases.

Mixed tickets support:
- 2–8 legs
- Safer, Balanced, or Spicy recipes
- Market selection
- Individual leg locks
- Rerolling only unlocked legs
- Fair odds, model hit rate, projected profit, Prop Score, projection, and reasons
