# V15 feature map

## Home
- Six highest-projected hitters
- HR probability and Dinger Score
- Two simple projection reasons
- Opposing probable pitcher, hand, and HR/9 when announced
- Weather/ballpark directional impact
- Matchup cards linked to Team Sheets

## Team Sheets
- Highest projected HR matchup opens first
- Clickable matchup tabs for the remaining games
- Both teams on the same sheet
- Dinger Score, HR probability, Last 7, and Last 15 status bars
- Opposing probable-pitcher details and prices

## Game Sims
- 5,000 deterministic simulations per game
- Projected score, hits, HR, strikeouts, stolen bases, and win probability
- Uses MLB team season production, the connected hitter board, and pitcher adjustments

## Parlay Lab
- Balanced, Longshot, Pipedream, and Blend modes
- Lock individual players
- Reroll only unlocked legs
- Blend high-probability anchors with long-price legs

## Data styling
- Dark neon charts and tables
- Bright, bold readable text
- Cyan, pink, green, yellow, purple, and red accents
